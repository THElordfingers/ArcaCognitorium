# MODUS OPERATI NOMENCLATURAE

### *The Binding Law of Canonical Terminology*
*Nomenclatura Arcana — Cogniverse Naming Authority*
*Issued: 2026-04-04*

---

## I. PURPOSE

This document is the operative law governing how terms are coined, proposed,
ratified, used, amended, and retired within the Cogniverse. It is binding on
The Builder, on all future sessions, and on any system or document produced
under the authority of the Arca Cognitorium.

It does not describe how naming has been done. It prescribes how naming
must be done henceforth.

---

coined without first identifying its stratum. Hybrid strata (e.g. I+II) are
permitted when morphologically justified.

## III. THE TWO-WORD PRINCIPLE

Default to two-word names.

- One word = a label
- Two words = a title
- Three words = cosmic scope only (reserved for things of the magnitude of
  the Machinae Mundi Lapsus)

Exceptions exist and are ratified as such. The Notice Board is one word
pairs and earns its exception by deliberate anti-Nomenclatura character.
Exceptions do not set precedent.

## VI. THE NAMING PROCEDURE

When a new name is required, the following procedure is mandatory. No step
may be skipped.

1. IDENTIFY the stratum — I, II, III, or justified hybrid
2. SELECT the pattern — two words default, three only for cosmic scope
3. PROPOSE the candidate — do not treat it as canonical
4. MARK it explicitly as a candidate in all documents
5. PRESENT it to the Wizard for ratification
6. RECORD the ratification — in the Ratification Card format, with date,
   session, and seal
7. DEPOSIT in the Master Register

Until step 6 is complete, a term has no canonical standing regardless of
how widely it has been used informally.

The Builder proposes. The Wizard ratifies. The Tower remembers.

## VII. THE RATIFICATION CARD

Every ratified term receives a Ratification Card. The card format is
governed by the companion schema:

```
RatificationCard_Schema.json
```

Required fields on every card:

- `id` — zero-padded integer
- `canonical` — exact ratified form
- `stratum` — per Section II
- `category` — what this term names
- `definition` — what it is and does
- `status` — ratified / ratified_partial / sediment / back_catalogued /
  deferred
- `ratified_by` — always LordFingers, The Absent Architect
- `ratified_in` — the session or document
- `ratified_date` — ISO 8601
- `seal` — always ⟁

## VIII. ON ALIASES AND EPITHETS

Aliases and epithets are not competing canonical names. They must be
explicitly ratified as such and recorded on the card with type and scope.

Types:
- `shorthand` — abbreviated form of the canonical name
- `epithet` — honorary or descriptive title
- `contextual` — name used in a specific semantic context
- `mystical_epithet` — evocative title with cosmological resonance
- `common_use` — acceptable informal form
- `subtitle` — secondary name in a Title: Subtitle construction

An alias does not become canonical by frequency of use. Ratification is
required.

---

## IX. AMENDMENT AND RETIREMENT

A ratified term may be amended or retired only by explicit Wizard ruling
during a formal ratification session.

Amendment: The card is updated with the amendment and a revision note. The
original form is preserved in the card history.

Retirement: Terms are never deleted. They are assigned status `sediment`
and deposited in the Detritus Pipeline. The geological record of the
Cogniverse is permanent.

## XI. ON THE DEVOTED ABSURD REGISTER

The Devoted Absurd is a ratified compositional register, not a stratum.
It governs tone, not naming law. Its use is appropriate in the Compendium,
the Notice Board, and any context where Bureaucratic Fatalism serves the
cosmological character of the Cogniverse.

The Devoted Absurd does not exempt a term from the naming procedure. A
term coined in the Devoted Absurd register must still pass through
ratification.
