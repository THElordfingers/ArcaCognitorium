## I. PURPOSE

This document is the binding operational procedure governing the creation,
use, and ratification of all canonical terminology within the Cogniverse.
It does not replace the DECRETUM RATIFICATIONIS MAGNAE. It extends it —
providing the procedural substrate that keeps the register alive, accurate,
and growing without requiring a full RATIFICATIO MAGNA for every new term.

This document governs The Builder, Lexifer, and any future system that
participates in the naming of Cogniverse concepts. It is not a guideline.
It is operational law.

## III. THE NAMING PROCEDURE

When a new name is required in the Cogniverse — for any entity, system,
mechanic, space, concept, or phenomenon — the following procedure governs.

### Step 1 — Identify the Stratum

Before proposing a name, identify which stratum it belongs to.

  — Is this a permanent, institutional, or cosmological concept? → Stratum I
  — Does it require an invented Latinate word for something new? → Stratum II
  — Is this an intimate space, an anomaly, or a self-aware moment? → Stratum III
  — Does it carry its plainness as a deliberate statement? → English, noted

Hybrid strata are permitted when the name draws from multiple registers.
The stratum is always declared.

### Step 2 — Select the Pattern

Default to two-word constructions. One word is a label. Two words are a title.
Three words are reserved for cosmic scope only.

### Step 3 — Propose the Name

The Builder proposes. The name is presented as a candidate. It is marked
as such. It does not enter the register. It lives in the LORIDEX.

The proposal includes:

  — The candidate name
  — The stratum
  — The functional category
  — A brief definition
  — Any competing terms or discrepancies found in existing usage
  — A flag if the term is underdressed for the register it occupies

### Step 4 — Present to the Wizard

Candidates are presented to the Wizard for ruling. Presentation is done
in the Ratificatio style — one term at a time, with any discrepancies
named before a ruling is requested.

The Wizard confirms, amends, or rejects.

  — Confirmed → proceeds to Step 5
  — Amended → revised candidate returns to Step 3
  — Rejected → struck from consideration, noted in the record

### Step 5 — Ratify and Register

On Wizard confirmation, the term is:

  1. Assigned a term_id in the master register
  2. Written as a full CHARTA RATIFICATIONIS card
  3. Added to the NOMENCLATURA ARCANA MASTER REGISTER JSON
  4. Marked with the SIGNUM VERIFIX
  5. Countersigned by The Builder

The term is now canonical. The Tower remembers it.

## IV. AMENDMENT PROCEDURE

A ratified term may be amended. Amendments do not erase the prior record.
The amendment is dated, noted, and the register updated.

Amendment triggers:

  — Spelling correction
  — Stratum reassignment
  — Definition refinement
  — Addition of epithets or aliases
  — Addition of banned forms
  — Reclassification to sediment or struck status

Amendment procedure:

  1. The Builder surfaces the amendment need, naming the specific field
     and the proposed change
  2. The Wizard confirms
  3. The register is updated with amendment date and note
  4. The original ratification date is preserved

No amendment is made without explicit Wizard confirmation.

## V. THE JUDICIUM EXLORICA PIPELINE

This section describes the future ratification path — not yet operative,
but binding in specification for when the Judicium Exlorica is built.

When Exvacua Loricum is operational and the Judicium Exlorica ceremony
has been implemented, terminology ratification will migrate to the
following path:

  1. Terminology observations are emitted via Nuntius as Involucrum
     payloads to Exvacua Loricum
  2. Actio Interpretus processes observations into Lorixii Speculativum
  3. Lorixii are clustered by domain and presented at Judicium Exlorica
  4. The five Phasis of the ceremony govern Wizard interaction:
     — Phasis I — Obscuranda Necessitum — allow/deny individual Lorixii
     — Phasis II — Colloquium Elucidativum — Wizard elaborates, corrects
     — Phasis III — Ostensio Loridexii — Loridex Card drafted and presented
     — Phasis IV — Exlorica Methodicum — Exloricum drafted, final edits
     — Phasis V — Sacramentum Finalitus — Wizard ratifies, written to canon
  5. Loretic Crystalizer updates the Loricum Ratifex
  6. The NOMENCLATURA ARCANA MASTER REGISTER JSON is updated to reflect
     the newly ratified term
  7. A CHARTA RATIFICATIONIS card is generated and stamped

Until the Judicium Exlorica is operational, direct Wizard ruling via
RATIFICATIO MAGNA sessions is the canonical path. This founding decree
is the precedent. Future sessions follow the same format.

## VI. ON THE USE OF CANONICAL TERMS

Canonical terms must be used in their ratified forms in:

  — All documents produced for the Cogniverse (Expositio, Dux Tome, .wiz)
  — All entity YAML files (role.yaml, traits.yaml, lore.yaml, celestial.yaml)
  — All UI strings within the Tower and Exocognii applications
  — All lore content, Compendium entries, and COMPENDICANA
  — All session communications between The Builder and The Wizard
  — All code comments and variable names where a canonical term is named

The system-facing register may use English or simplified forms in code
variables and filenames. The Wizard-facing register must always use the
canonical form.
