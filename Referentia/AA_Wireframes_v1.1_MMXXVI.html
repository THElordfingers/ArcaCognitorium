WF-03

Nesting Visual Standard

Containment Accuracy Mandate
Convocatio Amendment — Wizard Directive · Non-negotiable

A container with nested objects must accurately portray the relative size, shape, and orientation of all contained objects within the parent's visible boundaries. Children are rendered at their registered canvas sizes, positioned relative to their parent's origin, and clipped within the parent's rect. Snap-to-parent must be unambiguous. Anything short of this is non-functional.

Snap-to-parent mechanics
Drop detection algorithm
1. Drop fires at scene_pos from event.position().toPoint()
2. scene.items(scene_pos) queried → list ordered front-to-back
3. First item whose element.accepts_children() == True is the parent candidate
4. If candidate found: new element's graphics_item.setParentItem(candidate.graphics_item)
5. Position set relative to parent origin, snapped to 16px grid
6. Child moves with parent. Child clipped within parent rect.
7. serialize() records x/y as relative offsets from parent origin
8. deserialize() reconstructs with setParentItem + relative setPos
Relative sizingChildren rendered at their registered sizes from element_sizes.py. Not uniform 200×100.
ClippingsetParentItem() enforces Qt scene graph clipping. Children cannot overflow parent rect visually.
Round-tripx/y stored as relative offsets in serialize(). Deserialize reconstructs hierarchy cleanly.

Two separate PreviewRenderer instances share a single DesignDataModel. The feature page instance lives inside the Scriptorium Canvas. The floating window instance lives in a detached QMainWindow. Amendment

Dirty State Marker Proposals

Asterisk Retired · Wizard Ratifies at v1.1 Review
Convocatio Amendment — Asterisk Retired · Three Proposals Submitted

The asterisk (*) is retired as the dirty state indicator across all bureau applications. Three alternatives are proposed for Wizard ratification. All three appear in the Titulum beside the canvas name. The Wizard selects one; it becomes canonical across all three bureaus.

✦ Builder's Recommendation
Proposal A — Luminous Dot
CANVAS · nav_panel  ●
A small glowing dot in C_GOLD. Present when dirty. Absent when clean. No text — a sigil, not a label. The canvas name is unchanged. The mark appears after it, breathing.
Proposal B — Scriptum Sigil
CANVAS · nav_panel  ⊙
The circle-dot glyph ⊙ — a solar mark in C_GOLD_DIM when dirty, fully absent when clean. Carries symbolic weight without being a status indicator. Feels discovered, not announced.
Proposal C — Unsaved Label
CANVAS · nav_panel
non servata
A micro italic Latin phrase beneath the canvas name. "Non servata" — unsaved. Fully aligned with the register. Heavier than the other proposals; appropriate for those who prefer legibility over glyphs.
The Builder submits Proposal A. The Wizard ratifies. Whatever is chosen propagates to Bureau I and Bureau III without exception.
