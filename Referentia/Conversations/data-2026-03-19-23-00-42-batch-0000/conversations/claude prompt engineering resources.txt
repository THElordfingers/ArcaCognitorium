There isn't a single dedicated Claude prompt engineering podcast, but here are 
the best options depending on what you're after:

**Closest to what you want:**

- **Lex Fridman Podcast #452** — He interviews Amanda Askell, an Anthropic 
researcher who worked on Claude's alignment and fine-tuning, described as 
someone who has probably spoken with Claude more than anyone at Anthropic. She 
covers practical prompt engineering advice directly from the source.

- **AWS for Software Companies Podcast, Ep050** — Anthropic's Zach Witten 
goes deep on Claude-specific prompt engineering tips, covering system prompts, 
XML tags, chain-of-thought, and role setting.

- **The Startup Ideas Podcast** — A December 2025 episode specifically covers 
10 prompting rules drawn directly from Anthropic's own guidance on how to use 
Claude effectively, including structured formatting, constraints, and "power 
phrases."

**If you want to go deeper on the engineering side:**

- **The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck** — Has an episode with Boris Cherny, 
creator of Claude Code at Anthropic, going into how Claude's agentic approach 
works and how Claude.md memory files let you build a living knowledge base for 
prompting.

**Honestly the best non-podcast resource** is Anthropic's own documentation at 
`docs.claude.com` — their prompt engineering guide is thorough and 
Claude-specific. For your workflow across LuxArcana, ESTUPIDO, and your other 
projects, it's worth having that open as a reference since a lot of the XML tag 
and system prompt patterns apply directly to Python/API usage.
