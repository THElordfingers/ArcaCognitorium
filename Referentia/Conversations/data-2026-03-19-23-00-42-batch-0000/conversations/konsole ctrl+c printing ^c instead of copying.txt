Claude:  Ah, you've accidentally switched Konsole into a mode where it's interpreting 
Ctrl+C literally as a character sequence rather than as a copy command!

This happens when you hit **Ctrl+Shift+C** by mistake. In most terminal 
emulators including Konsole:

- **Ctrl+Shift+C** = Copy (in terminal mode)
- **Ctrl+Shift+V** = Paste (in terminal mode)
- **Ctrl+C** = Send interrupt signal (cancel running command)

When you see `^C` appearing in your terminal, Konsole is working normally - 
that's actually the correct behavior for Ctrl+C in a terminal. The `^C` 
represents the interrupt signal being sent to whatever program is running.

If you want to copy text in Konsole, you need to:
1. Select the text with your mouse
2. Press **Ctrl+Shift+C** (not just Ctrl+C)

Or you can simply select text and it will automatically copy to your clipboard 
if you have that option enabled in Konsole's settings.

If your terminal is somehow in a weird state where the shortcuts aren't working 
properly, you can try:
- Closing and reopening Konsole
- Checking Settings → Configure Keyboard Shortcuts to verify your key bindings
- Resetting your profile if the issue persists
