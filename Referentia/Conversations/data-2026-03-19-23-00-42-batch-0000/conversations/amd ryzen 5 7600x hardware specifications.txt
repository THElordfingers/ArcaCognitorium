Great question! Here's how it works:

## 🎯 Simple Version

**Think of it like this:**

1. **Model = Compressed Brain** - A 6GB file containing 8 billion learned 
patterns
2. **Loaded into Your RAM** - Sits in your 64GB memory (uses ~20GB total with 
context)
3. **You Type** → Model thinks → **Responds word-by-word**
4. **Each word takes ~30-50ms** to calculate on your CPU
5. **Result:** 20-30 words per second streaming out

## 🔄 What's Actually Happening

```
You: "Explain quantum computing"
     ↓
[Tokenizer] Breaks into: ["Explain", "quantum", "computing"]
     ↓
[Model in RAM] Processes 8 billion parameters
     ↓ (30ms per word)
Model: "Quantum" → "computing" → "is" → "a" → ...
     ↓
You see: "Quantum computing is a revolutionary..."
```

**Key Points:**
- **Each word calculated separately** based on all previous words
- **Uses your CPU** (all 12 threads working)
