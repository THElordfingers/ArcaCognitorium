system around that family: *Quis, Quid, Quando, Ubi, Cur.*

| Latin | Meaning/Feel |
|---|---|

LordFingers: "list" "view" "origin" "thread/conversations"

Claude:  | Action/Concept | Latin |
|---|---|
| List | **Index** / **Catalogus** / **Recensio** |
| View | **Visio** / **Spectа** / **Prospectus** |
| Origin | **Origo** / **Fons** (source/spring) / **Genesis** |
| Thread / Conversations | **Filum** (thread, literal) / **Colloquia**

---

**Standouts:**

- **Index** — already Latin, already perfect
- **Fons** — beautiful for origin, implies a living source rather than just a 
starting point
- **Filum** — "thread" literally, and the root of *filament*; very evocative 
for a conversation chain
- **Colloquia** — plural of *colloquium*, dignified and exact

**Filum** and **Colloquia** could work together — *Fila* (plural, threads) 
for the list view, *Colloquium* for an individual conversation.

LordFingers: "Council" "Lore" "story" "backstory"

Claude:  | Concept | Latin |
|---|---|
| Council | **Concilium** / **Senatus** / **Consilium** (counsel/deliberation) |
| Lore | **Lore** / **Doctrina** / **Sapientia** / **Eruditio** |
| Story | **Fabula** (tale/narrative) / **Historia** / **Narratio** |
| Backstory | **Origo** / **Ante Fabula** / **Historia Prima** / **Radix**

---

**Standouts:**

- **Consilium** — for council, but with the added sense of *deliberation and 
wisdom*, not just a group
- **Doctrina** — lore as a body of teaching; weighty and arcane
- **Fabula** — story with a mythic/told-aloud quality, the root of *fable*
- **Radix** — for backstory, meaning *root*; implies the hidden foundation 
beneath the surface narrative

**Radix** is especially strong for backstory in a mystical/RPG context — it 
suggests something buried and foundational rather than just "what happened 
before."
