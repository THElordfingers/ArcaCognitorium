Fat trimmed, muscles kept strong. But the Distillation makes judgment calls.

reads absence?

---

**The Residuum Ghost**
An item breaks. Residuum is collected. But Residuum is the ghost of what the 
item was. At sufficient accumulation, at sufficient age — Residuum isn't just 
a crafting material anymore. It's concentrated *history.* What does 
concentrated history do when left alone long enough in a warm kitchen?

---

The Aura feeds feature unlocks. Feature unlocks change behavior. Changed 
behavior feeds new Reflection signals. New signals reshape the Aura. That loop

tower has to respond to the noise.
