The crop tool in Spectacle (especially the version that came with Plasma 6.1+) 
has a bit of a non-obvious confirm step. Here's how it works:

**After drawing your crop box:**

1. **Double-click inside the selection** — this is the primary way to confirm 
and apply the crop.
2. **Or press Enter** — same effect, commits the crop.

If neither of those is responding, a few things worth checking:

- Make sure you're actually in the **Crop tool**, not the Selection tool. They 
look similar but behave differently — the Selection tool is for selecting 
existing annotations to move them, not for cropping the image itself.
- The crop tool was rebuilt and re-introduced in Plasma 6.1, integrated 
internally rather than relying on a third-party annotations library. If your 
Spectacle version predates that, the crop tool may be present in the toolbar 
but buggy or non-functional.

**To check your Spectacle version:**
```bash
spectacle --version
```

On Debian Trixie you should be reasonably current, but it's worth confirming. 
If you're on a version from before Plasma 6.1 era, the crop tool was genuinely 
broken/missing for a period — it was lost after a library change and there 
was active community pressure to get it restored.

If double-click and Enter both fail, the workaround that actually works 
reliably is using **rectangular region capture mode** — take the screenshot 
by dragging a region selection *during capture* rather than cropping afterward. 
That gives you the exact area without needing the post-capture crop tool.

-selection clipboard` targets

PRIMARY by default, which won't interfere with your `Ctrl+C` clipboard at all. 
So you could just drop the `-selection clipboard` flag from the tee pipe and 
your two clipboards stay completely independent.
