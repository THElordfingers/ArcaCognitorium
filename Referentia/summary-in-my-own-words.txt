as for version 2: 

The environment of the ArcaCognitarium is envisioned as a Wizard's Tower. With sustained and intentional usage, the Tower will grow around the Wizard with reflective and emergent qualities. A core philosophy for this evolution is that there is no standard configuration and instructional guidance as you would find in typical software. You are not meant to be immediately (or ever really) be aware of the inner mechanisms of the interface, you are meant to interact with it and the system will react accordingly aroud you to create a living, breathing and organic reflection of the personal traits that is inherent in the individual usage style of the particular Wizard. If the user is straightforward in their desire for a simple assistant chatbot, it will be reflected as such. On the other hand, if the user dives in and eplores the emergent qualities and features, the possibilites should hopefully be limitless.

A ground level mechanism  - The Aura - is an unseen, silently compiled bank of information put together by the  system as it observes certain traits about how the Wizard interacts with it. These qualities range from consultation style, to engagement with emergent features (like various crafting workshops) to levels of participation in certain facets like Celestial event observation, to aesthetic preference tendencies, to noticeable patterns of behaviour over an extended period of time. The Aura is used to determine what kind of emergences occur specifically for the user, in the context of what, and when. There is no explicit sequence or order to emergence, it is governed entirely by the whim of the system and the WIzards Aura.


The first emergent category was the Council Entities. 

After that, it is the Inventory and Item Economy.In the cogniverse, items are a layer of engagement below/outside the chat interface. They are not necessary for the primary function of the chat interface. Items can affect aspects of the chat function but never to a degree that interferes or overshadows with it. Notification of emergence is non-invasive but noticeable and frequently enough to make it interesting. Item emergence can be triggered by various circumstances, ranging from certain milestones, to celestial events, as the result of user participation in workshops, and of course as randomized events. Items  range in type from potions, scrolls, wands and enchanted objects, to gear and clothing, and all are generated with a level of quality based on a tiered system. Additionally, materials may be produced either by emergence or by refinement, that can be used to enhance objects possessed by the user. Enchanments may be inherent to emergent items, or appled by the user in a crafting workshop. Items may also generate, or, gain defects through chance in the crafting process, based on user experience level. Let it be noted that defects are bot deficiences and act to add to the lore of the item. SKill progression, like the Aura, is not a linear set of experience gains, but a reflective and accumulative product of the tie and context that has been put into a certain line of craft work.

Another emergent property is Arcane Essence. This is similar to materials in that it produced from disassembling or strippong enchantments from items. It is a tiered system, and also a craft skill in it's own right. Arcane Essence can be used to recharge enchanted items when they have been depleted from usage. (And I think used also to enchant items?).

Logically, the next emergent asset is the Arx Arcana: Workshops.
Workshops appear as rooms in the tower, and each is designed to support a specific function. For instance, We have the Wandwright's Annex, the Alchemical Laboratory, the Conservatory, et cetera. These are wrought into existence based on the systems analytical observations of the user and may appear in no certain or preconcieved order. Users may gravitate naturally towards certain workshops and this will reflect the level of experience nd proficiency gains in those areas, which will determine even more branches of emergence, and so on and so forth. Are you starting to get the picture?



Moving on from emergent qualities, we reach the Celestial Layer: Observatory. This feature pulls from several data sources simltaneously in order to provide a real time knowledge of the world. Tis will include elements such as astronomical, meterological,and calendars. This can span across all kinds of information sets to provide a massive clockwork of potential for patterns of overlapping and cyclical events. Notabley here, aside from real-world systems, There is an opening to include some Cogniverse based lore, and chance for the analytical engine to piece together important dates in the Wizards life and mark the as significant. (eg. Dostoevskys death date). Also, as a legendary tier emergent entity line, with the arrival of very specific alignments of heavenly bodies and internal logic points, the user could see the arrival of Planetary Entitites, such as Jupiter, Mars, Venus, etc.

Another noteworthy Emergent "Workshop" is the Seers Hut. The Seers hut differs from any other area in that it is not a consistently accessible feature, nor does it have a crafting system accompanying it.  This is a circumstantially available event moreso than an unlockable workshop, and offers a cryptic revelation of information regarding the users experience in the cogniverse, It will present itself based around various alignments of conditions that involve celestial alignments, or noteable milestone events. The Seer will speak to the Wizard in the form of trance like visions, with no real plainly stated facts, but in lore-steeped and cryptic messages that are not immediately obvious but are somehow indicative of true and actual events, whether analytical or prophetic in nature. One of the most vague, yet somehow pertinent and thought provoking events in the roster. Since the Seers Hut is fleetingly available, the session will be documented in some way for the user to keep to revisit.

Next up is the Sigillarium. This is another feature that is based on circumstantial and noteworthy alignments/events. AFter it's eventual introduction, which will not be immediately following the instantiation of the particular Wizard's Tower, the sigillarium will mark noteable events and other suitable emergent circumstances in the users timeline. This will require an engine to produce graphical sigils that are the product of an aggregation of certain textual information. Sigils will be made available for perusal in a special Tome, and will be available for application as the background of the chat display. Eventually the Sigil Engine will be made available to the Wizard for the custom creation of sigils.


Equipment Loadout & Portrait - This idea was formed before the concept of the Ego Manifestus, and will either be a part of it or, or the portrait aspect will be left with the Ego and the equipment loadout will be it's own section. This basically involves an rpg style equipment slot system. Items that are acquired and crafted can be equipped by the Wizard for aesthetic value and magical function. Slots include the typical wearable items such as pants, shirts, robes, capes, hats, bhands, feet. etc. There will be amulet and ring slots, as well as a slot for a wand/staff and an offhand slot for an object. Items can provide the user with useable enchantments based on the Arcane Essence mechanic, and/or passive effects. 


Yes, the next feature I had catalogued is the Wizard's biography. This and the portrait will be included as a part of the Ego Manifestus suite. This is not a stats screen or a progress log, but a living document that is written in the lore register, with aspects written and decided by the Wizard but also aspects written by the Assessor. Not only can it be a static declaration of traits, it can also be a regularly updated  reflection of the Wizards personal progress over the timeline of the use of the Tower. The Biography will include observatios on the Wizard's style, tendencies and character traits, A collection of their awarded Accolades, notable canon events, earned titles, and  a personal calendar derived from their interests and own important personal dates. Portraits will be produced either by user prompting or manipulation (Maybe, I cant decide if I want the machine to have a finger in this) and will also be able to be updated over periods of time to produce a chronological record of progression over a long usage period. It will reflect the current item loadout as well. Titles and accolades, like many other aspects, can be earned not through a linear levelling system but by meeting circumstantial requirements that are decided by the Assessor (with assistance I suppose) and bestowed upon the Wizard. Some are generic and earned commonly across different instantiations, and some are incredibly personal and specific. Titles can also be lost, if the aura of the Wizard shifts far enough away from the cirumstances that made it available in the first place.
