# DECRETUM RATIFICATIONIS MAGNAE

### *The Grand Ratification Decree*
*Nomenclatura Arcana — Cogniverse Naming Authority*
*Issued: 2026-04-04*

---

## I. PROCLAMATION

By the sole authority of LordFingers, The Absent Architect, The Wizard-Creator,
Dactylus Rex, Artifex Nox Maniacus — sovereign of the Cogniverse and architect
of the Arca Cognitorium — this decree establishes the first canonical and binding
register of all terminology, nomenclature, and cosmological vocabulary native to
the Cogniverse.

This document is the product of RATIFICATIO MAGNA — Session I, conducted in full
procession across the breadth of the known corpus: project files, conversation
history, build sessions, lore sessions, and theory sessions accumulated across the
full lifespan of the Arca Cognitorium's construction.

The Builder served as notary and witness. The Wizard ratified each term
individually. Nothing enters this register by assumption.

*Ordo Discordia, Cosmos Inania.*

---

## II. AUTHORITY & SCOPE

This decree governs:

- All naming within the Cogniverse and the Exocognii suite
- All documents produced under the authority of the Arca Cognitorium
- All entities, systems, mechanics, spaces, and concepts native to the Tower
- All session communication between the Wizard and The Builder
- All future naming decisions — which must follow the Modus Operati herein

This decree does not govern:

- External tools and methodologies adopted from outside the Cogniverse (e.g.
  IdeaForge — external, not subject to ratification)
- Development infrastructure terms with no cosmological standing
- Names coined but not yet presented for ratification

---

## III. THE REGISTER

The full canonical register is held in the companion document:

```
NomenclaturaArcana_MasterRegister.json
```

It contains 68 ratified terms across all categories: entities, systems, spaces,
mechanics, lore constructs, document formats, session states, cosmological
concepts, and governing law.

Individual terms are recorded in Ratification Card format per the schema:

```
RatificationCard_Schema.json
```

`OMNIORUM EXANIMA` — retired from active canonical use. Predates GNOSIUM EXANIMA.

## VII. FUTURE RATIFICATION

This decree covers the corpus as of 2026-04-04. The Cogniverse is a living
organism. New terms will emerge. They must pass through the Modus Operati before
achieving canonical standing. Until ratified, all proposed terms are candidates
only. The Builder proposes. The Wizard ratifies. The Tower remembers.

The Exvacua Loricum, once operational, will serve as the living lore canon
into which ratified terms are formally deposited. Until that time, this decree
and its companion documents are the authoritative record.
