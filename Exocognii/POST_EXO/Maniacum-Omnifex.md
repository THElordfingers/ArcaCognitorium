

MANIACUM OMNIFEX


    You awake from a week long flurry of late night THEORIA ET ARCANA and weekend marathon AEDIFICARE sessions, to 
the foundational scatterings of an industrial village at the door of your Tower. You blink and cough away the 
stranded remnants of asynchronous thought processes and noxious mental accelerants, and attempt to survey the 
RELIQUIAE OPERIS and make some sense of it. 

    Several Institutions have taken  form, seemingly overnight. You can see scores of oddly disheveled bureaucratic 
minions milling about, in a subdued, but determined attempt to portray their lack of INERTIA. Devoted, yet absurd.
Their dirty, brightly coloured overcoats make a strange contrast with the grim desaturated hues of the heaps of off 
cast construction detritus littering the freshly rendered landscape. 


Ordo Discordia. Cosmos Inania. 


    First, it would be wise to take stock of your immediacy. What new APPARATUS ARCANORUM hath thou forged in the wake
of your fervour? 
You survey your workspace and find the usual fragmented remains of your EFFUSIONES VEHEMENTES: half pieces together 
documents and  glyph syllabuses, a multitude of TABULA ARCANUM. Nothing out of the ordinary.

  Under a sheaf of glowing panels, oddly translucent and lacking a third dimension, you uncover something you haven't 
seen before. It is unfamiliar, but clearly carries your distinct magical stylizings.  A  rectangular control panel 
with a vast array of switches, dials, lights, displays and holographics. It seems largely disorganized, with 
interchangeable panels clustered in a chaotic mess of disrepair and clearly not in operation. A faceplate on the top 
reads PRAESIDIUM.
