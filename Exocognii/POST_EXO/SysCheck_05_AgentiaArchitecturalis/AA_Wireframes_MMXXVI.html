Overview

The Tabula Designandi is the primary design surface. Elements are dragged from the Elementarium and placed spatially on the canvas. Containers accept children dropped within their bounds. The Inspector governs properties; the Specularium shows the rendered truth.

Nesting

Drop any element onto a container (QFrame, QGroupBox, QSplitter, QTabWidget) and it becomes a child. The graphics item is reparented — it moves with the container and is spatially constrained within it. Resize children via the Inspector.
