On build order — the Color Theme Builder first. It's the smallest,
most self-contained, and everything downstream depends on theme.json
existing. Getting it built and producing a ratified palette unblocks both
other tools immediately. UI Aesthetic Theme Builder second — it's the
one you'll use most during active Tower development, so it earns its place
early. The Bureau third — it's the largest and least urgent as a development
dependency. Documents can be produced manually until it exists. The order is:
foundation, then most used, then most complex.
