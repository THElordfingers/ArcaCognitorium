

  1. First I'll show you show simple weaving techniques.
     Pick a line weight, right now it's not important but later
     Your choices can make a great effect on your flow.
     Make a square.

        ┋             ┋
        ┋             ┋
        ┋             ┋
        ┋             ┋

        ┋             ┋
		┗╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍┛



  2. Now, I switch to a solid line weight. The choice is again arbitrary.
     I make a second square, so I can conjoin them in a nicely balanced interlink.
     



        ┏╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍┓
        ┋             ┋
        ┋             ┋   One line from the second square goes "under" the line of the other square.
        ┋             ┋  
        ┋          ┏━━┋━━━━━━━━━━┓
        ┋          ┃  ┋          ┃
        ┋          ┃  ┋          ┃
        ┗╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍╍┃╍╍┛          ┃
                   ┃             ┃
                   ┃             ┃
                   ┃             ┃
                   ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛


       The next line goes "over" the line of the first square.




  As a rule, lines should always try to follow an "over, under, over under rule.
